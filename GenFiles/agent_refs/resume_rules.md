# Resume Rules

Load this when writing or revising tailored resume XML.

- Prefer one page when the strongest relevant content fits cleanly.
- Allow a second page when important relevant content would otherwise be removed.
- Avoid sparse or filler-based two-page resumes.
- Lead with a summary aligned to the target role.
- Reorder skills and selected entries for the strongest supported fit.
- Include at least two employment entries.
- Every selected project must have at least three bullets.
- Every selected employment entry must have at least three bullets.
- Prefer fewer well-developed entries over many thin entries.
- If trimming is needed, remove an entire less-relevant entry before reducing a
  selected entry below three bullets.
